## What's fixed

- Startup now reflects Windows' enabled or disabled startup state, includes boot and logon scheduled tasks, and lists the Windows desktop and sign-in components as protected system entries.
- Startup switches now update Windows' startup approval state; eligible current-user scheduled startup tasks can be enabled or disabled.
- Processes opens to current-user apps with a **Show all** switch for the full process list.
- The standby cleaner shows **Off** whenever automatic cleaning is disabled. The setting remains saved, and auto-clean stays off by default.
