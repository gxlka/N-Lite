## What's fixed

- Stop repeat setup prompts when cleaner settings or status contents cannot be parsed; setup is recognized from the protected helper/version marker and per-user settings file.
- The SYSTEM helper now rebuilds a corrupt or missing status file with safe defaults. N-Lite restores cleaner settings from saved preferences at startup.
- Updating from an older cleaner may ask for approval once to replace the protected helper. Manual and automatic cleaning then continue through the SYSTEM task without routine prompts.
